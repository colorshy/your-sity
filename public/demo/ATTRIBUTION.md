# Demo media

- `city.svg`, `books.svg`: original illustrations created for Your City. They are illustrative, not photos or verified representations of local architecture.
- `mountains.jpg`: example photograph from the MIT-licensed Next.js repository, downloaded from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vercel/next.js/canary/examples/image-component/public/mountains.jpg . Repository license: https://github.com/vercel/next.js/blob/canary/license.md . Used only as a clearly identified illustrative mountain landscape, not a photograph of Shymkent or a proposed route.

All media ships locally; the public demo needs no image CDN or API credentials.
